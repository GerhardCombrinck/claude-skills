\chapter{Structure}

A report-class document gets the chapter heading above: the label in the accent
colour, the title in the body face at display size. Nothing needs setting per
document.

\begin{houseinfo}
Use \texttt{houseinfo} for rationale and context: the paragraph a reader can
skip without losing the thread. Grey, quiet, no urgency.
\end{houseinfo}

\begin{housewarn}[The expensive mistake]

optional argument overrides the default title.
\end{housewarn}

\section{Reference tables}

Narrow fixed-width columns use the \texttt{L} column type, which is ragged
right. Justified text in a 4\,cm column produces rivers of whitespace.

\section{Diagrams}

Three node styles, two edge styles. Branch labels mask the line underneath.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[node distance=8mm and 14mm]
  \node[dgterm=44mm]                    (in)   {What comes in};
  \node[dgask=44mm, below=of in]         (ask)  {Is the thing true?};
  \node[dgbox=42mm, right=of ask]        (side) {Do the side thing};
  \node[dgterm=44mm, below=17mm of ask]  (out)  {The result};

  \draw[dgflow] (in) -- (ask);
  \draw[dgflow] (ask) -- (side) node[dgtag, pos=0.5] {yes};
  \draw[dgflow] (ask) -- (out)  node[dgtag, pos=0.5] {no};
  \draw[dgflow] (side.south) -- ++(0,-5mm) -| ([xshift=15mm]out.north);
\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Caption styling, the float pin, and every diagram style at once.}
\label{fig:flow}
\end{figure}

Figures are pinned with \texttt{[H]}. A diagram that drifts two pages away from
the paragraph explaining it is worse than a slightly short page.

\section{Alignment}

Boxes in a row are anchored by their top-left corners rather than centred, so
the tops align even when one box wraps onto extra lines and hangs lower.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[dgbox=29mm, anchor=north] (b1) at (0,0)
    {\textbf{One}\\[1pt]{\itshape short}};
  \node[dgbox=29mm, anchor=north west] (b2) at ([xshift=4mm]b1.north east)
    {\textbf{Two}\\[1pt]{\itshape rather more text here so it wraps onto
     extra lines}};
  \node[dgbox=29mm, anchor=north west] (b3) at ([xshift=4mm]b2.north east)
    {\textbf{Three}\\[1pt]{\itshape short}};
  \node[dgask=40mm, above=11mm of b2] (top) {Which one?};
  \draw[dgflow] (top.south) -- ++(0,-4mm) -| (b1.north);
  \draw[dgflow] (top.south) -- ++(0,-4mm) -| (b2.north);
  \draw[dgflow] (top.south) -- ++(0,-4mm) -| (b3.north);
\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Tops align; the taller box hangs lower.}
\end{figure}

Node padding is set separately for the horizontal and vertical axes. One
combined inner sep starves tall content (ceilings, fractions, big
parentheses) of depth, and the glyph collides with the bottom border:
$\left\lceil \textsf{quantity} \div \textsf{units} \right\rceil$.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[dgbox=52mm] {$\left\lceil \textsf{slots} \div 4 \right\rceil$ boxes\strut};
\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Ceiling delimiters clear of the border.}
\end{figure}

aside that should not compete with body text.}

\section{Where the rules live}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{3.6cm} X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{File} & \textbf{What it decides} \\
\midrule
\rowcolor{gymbg}
\texttt{house.tex}   & Every colour, font, macro and package. Edit once,
                       every document restyles. \\
\texttt{DIAGRAMS.md} & Node vocabulary and the layout rules: edge routing,
                       alignment, caption spacing. \\
\rowcolor{gymbg}
\texttt{LONGDOC.md}  & Chapters, callouts, captioned figures,
                       cross-referencing. \\
\texttt{EDITORS.md}  & Wiring the style into TeXworks, VS Code or Overleaf. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
