gc-skills

A LaTeX house style for Claude Code, and the documents it produces.

These are the sample documents from the claude-skills repository, served here so they open in your browser rather than downloading.

Sample documents

  • Shipping manual chapters, diagrams, appendices, glossary 38 pages
  • Report callouts, captioned figures, cross-references 2 pages
  • One-pager checklist tables, status chips, a flowchart 1 page

Install

In Claude Code: /plugin marketplace add GerhardCombrinck/claude-skills, then /plugin install gc-skills@invisionsoft.

Source and installation notes on GitHub. The company, rates and catalogue in the shipping manual are invented.
